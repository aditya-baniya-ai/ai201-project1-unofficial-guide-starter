DEPARTMENT: Biology — Texas State University

PROFESSOR: Ram Kumar Basanta
DEPARTMENT: Mathematics — Texas State University

REVIEW: Basanta is solid. His grading is fair — he gives partial credit on the work-shown sections. The practice problems he releases before exams are extremely helpful and closely mirror the actual tests. I felt prepared going into each exam.

REVIEW: Took Basanta for Calc II. He posts practice exam questions about a week before the real test and they are very similar to what shows up. If you do all of them and understand the steps you will do well.
